Origin of the big-endian fixtures: controlled test documents created by Robert
G. Patterson in Finale 2008 running under Mac OS X 10.4 hosted by QEMU, and
released by their author under this repository's MIT license. The same save
attempted under Mac OS X 10.7 in UTM crashed, which is why those specimens are
PowerPC ones.

The `BE` in the name is the point of it. Finale 2007 and 2008 straddle the Intel
transition and byte order varies across it; this is the big-endian Finale 2008
fixture, every other tracked zlib-era fixture later than Finale 2007 being
little-endian.

    The continuation of `tests/evidence/F2006/F2006-text-inserts.mus` into a
    release that has the inserts Finale 2006 lacked: one text block per insert,
    plus all seven File Info fields filled in. It answers three things that
    fixture could not.

    It fixes five more binary command codes. `^partname` arrives with Finale
    2007's linked parts and `^lyricist`, `^arranger` and `^subtitle` with Finale
    2008's fuller File Info, and all four are **appended** at 0xa0 to 0xa3 rather
    than slotted into the alphabetical run where their names would sort, which is
    how a release adds an insert without renumbering the ones already written
    into saved documents. That refutes a prediction made from the Finale 2006
    table and is the reason those predictions were never put into the reader.

    Its two `^time` blocks give the fifth code and a third argument width. They
    differ in one byte and are labeled "Time" and "Time with seconds", which is
    exactly musxdom's `^time` argument, and the byte is a **single** digit where
    every previously observed argument was four or eight. Finale 27 drops this
    insert on conversion, so the companion cannot corroborate the name; the
    controlled pair is the evidence, and the reader keeps the insert the
    conversion discards -- what Finale 27 drops on conversion says what that
    conversion does, not what the document contains.

    It also shows File Info leaving the header. All seven fields are `^fileInfo`
    records in the text pool, numbered by musxdom's own `FileInfoText::TextType`
    and confirmed field for field by the companion, while the header offsets that
    carry them in Finale 97 are empty. Where between Finale 2006 and Finale 2008
    the move happened is open, and the reader does not need to know: it fills from
    the header only what the pool did not supply.

    All seven TextBlock class records carry decimal 2004 at word 12 and refer to
    block text, confirming that the Finale 2006 text-family discriminator persists
    in the zlib class-record form.

    Two companion differences are intended. Its first block is the bare text
    `FULL SCORE` with no style commands at all, and Finale 27 writes
    `^fontid(1)^size(12)^nfx(0)FULL SCORE`; the reader keeps what the file says
    rather than synthesizing a font the document did not state. And Finale 27
    emits no `^time` at all, where the reader preserves it.
