    The big-endian counterpart to the Finale 2012 fixture. Finale 2007 is the
    transition release and most of its documents are big-endian. Every numeric
    field of the class-record encoding
    follows that byte order, including the 32-bit payload length in the record
    header. Composing that length from two 16-bit reads in a fixed word order
    yields an enormous value on such a file and aborts the record walk at the
    very first record, so the whole document silently yields nothing. This
    fixture is the regression test for that, and it carries nine font
    definitions.
