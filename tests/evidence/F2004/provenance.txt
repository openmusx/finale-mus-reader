F2004-lyropts-nosmart-wext.mus
    sha256 8dd9c16c98f4baac780cff135192143af1088db2083612919bf837e32dd04804
    From F2004-baseline, "Use Smart Word Extensions" switched off. Selector 34 word 5 moves 1
    to 0, the ETF prints ^34(65534) 0 0 0 0 0 0, and the companion loses
    <useSmartWordExtensions/>.
    The word exists in every era and means nothing before Finale 2004: it is 0 in every earlier
    document while every earlier companion switches the option on, and the Finale 2.6.3 fixture
    stores 12 there, which is no boolean at all. Reading it early would turn smart word
    extensions off for every Finale 2003-and-earlier document, so it is gated on selector 57
    exactly as the smart-hyphen switch on selector 35 is.

F2004-lyropts-needuscore.mus
    sha256 a825f2bbcdcd8b4433a7023b5e2a11470817bf3db93ba17fa83a69a6ad8b8aef
    From F2004-baseline, "Only Create on Lyrics With Underscores" switched on. Selector 57
    word 1 moves 0 to 1, the ETF prints ^57(65534) 1 1 38 1 0 0, and the companion gains
    <wordExtNeedUnderscore/>.
    The Finale 2004 dialog words this setting the opposite way round and unclearly, and Finale
    had reworded it to match the modern boolean by Finale 2012. The companion settles the sense
    rather than the wording: the stored 1 converts to the option being on, so the value needs no
    inversion.
    This also fills the last unexplained word of the selector 57 record, which had been noted as
    always zero and was once a candidate for holding a punctuation-list reference. Only word 5
    of that record remains unassigned.
    Both Finale 2004 saves also rewrote the word extension connection table as the dialog
    closed, giving its elements vertical offsets of 5, which is the same normalization the
    Finale 2012 and 3.7.2 lyric saves show.
